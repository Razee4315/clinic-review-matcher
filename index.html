Regulator notice check

When a regulator acts against a clinic, a clinic guide should notice. This small tool checks that.

The problem

In July 2026 Korea's Fair Trade Commission ordered three plastic surgery clinics to correct reviews that were paid for with discounts and not disclosed. A guide that gives those clinics a high rating has no way to know.

What this does

It reads the notice, finds the clinic pages it is about, and checks whether each page was last reviewed before or after the notice.

How it decides

  • Exact match on a cleaned name. Words like "plastic surgery", "clinic" and 성형외과 are removed, then the rest must be identical. No fuzzy matching, because these names are short and a wrong match would put a notice on the wrong clinic.
  • District as a second check. Name and district agreeing is high confidence. A different district blocks the match until someone checks the address.
  • A person approves every notice. The tool only prepares the work.
  • Dates decide the queue. A page reviewed before the notice is out of date.

What it does not do

  • It does not recalculate ratings or remove reviews. That needs the full review data and a policy decision.
  • It does not fetch notices by itself yet. This notice was entered by hand.
  • The clinic details are a small sample read from public clinic pages on 4 October 2026.
