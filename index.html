Regulator notice check

A clinic directory is only as trustworthy as its newest information. This prototype takes a regulator notice, finds the clinic pages it is about, and flags the ones that were last reviewed before the notice came out.

Try a name

Type a clinic name the way a news article or a Korean source might write it, for example DA Plastic Surgery Clinic, 뷰성형외과 or View Dental.

How it decides

  • Exact match on a cleaned name. Words like "plastic surgery", "clinic" and 성형외과 are removed, then the rest must be identical. There is no fuzzy matching, because these names are short and a wrong match would put a regulator notice on the wrong clinic.
  • District as a second check. Name and district agreeing is high confidence. A name match with a different district is blocked until someone checks the address.
  • A person approves every notice. The tool only prepares the work. Nothing reaches a clinic page automatically.
  • Dates decide the queue. A page reviewed before the notice is out of date and goes back for review.

What it does not do

  • It does not recalculate ratings or remove reviews. That needs the full review data and a policy decision.
  • It does not fetch notices by itself yet. The notice here was entered by hand from a published news report.
